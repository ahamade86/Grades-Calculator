Grade Calculator

This feature allows students to input their scores to calculate their running average and determine the average score needed to achieve specific grades such as an A or B. The results are based on the accuracy and completeness of the data entered by the user. The calculated targets are intended to serve as goals and do not guarantee a specific grade outcome. Various factors, including exam difficulty, grading policies, and individual performance, can influence final grades.

Probability Calculator

This feature estimates the probability of achieving a specific target grade based on current performance data and statistical methods. The probability estimates are based on the assumption that future performance will follow a normal distribution and that past performance is indicative of future results. These estimates are not guarantees and should be used as a guide rather than a definitive prediction. Users should consider additional factors such as changes in study habits, external influences, and individual effort when interpreting the results.

How the probability is calculated

Your average (mean) and standard deviation come from your graded scores. The Z-score is Z = (X - mean) / SD, where X is the average needed for the target grade. The probability is 1 minus the standard normal distribution value at Z.
